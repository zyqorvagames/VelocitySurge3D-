HOW TO PLAY

Steer: Use ◀ / ▶ or the A / D keys.

RACE: Hold the green RACE button or press W / ↑ to accelerate.

BRAKE: Hold BRAKE or press S / ↓ to slow down and stop.

Coins: Collect golden coins to unlock faster cars. Your total coins are saved between races.

View: Use the car icon during gameplay to switch cockpit and chase views.
